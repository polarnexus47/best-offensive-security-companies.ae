Editorial Policy

This policy governs every page of the ranking of offensive security companies in the UAE. It is written for readers who want to know how a statement got onto the page.

This is an independent comparison compiled by the Editorial Team from public sources, with no paid placements.

Last updated: November 2026

Sourcing rules

  1. Company facts come from the company. Services, offices and stated accreditations come from the company's own website on the day of review.
  2. Accreditations come from the issuer where one exists. CREST accreditation is read from the CREST Marketplace, and programme registrations from the programme's published list. If only the company states it, the label says so.
  3. Rules come from regulators. UAE regulations and standards are cited from the regulator's or standards body's own page. Law-firm summaries, blogs and third-party ranking pages are not used as evidence.
  4. Statistics carry context. Each statistic names its year, region and primary source. A figure from an earlier year is not presented as current.
  5. No evidence, no statement. If a fact cannot be verified, the page shows "Not stated" or omits it. It is never filled from memory or inferred from a competitor's page.

Equal treatment of companies

Every company profile uses the same template: six fixed fields, a body text and an official-site link in the same format. No company has extra sections, quotes or case studies. Company links are shown only in the ranking table and the profiles, and every link is a plain official-site URL.

Guides and trust pages link to regulators, standards bodies and internal pages. They do not link to company websites.

Corrections and company requests

Anyone can report an error to editor@best-offensive-security-companies.ae. A correction needs the page, the statement and a public source, such as the company's own page or an issuing directory. When the source supports the correction, the page is updated and its date changes.

Companies can ask for a correction or for a review of their own profile. The ranking order follows the ordering rule in the methodology, so a request to change position is not accepted.

Each review checks the same items for every company: the offensive service list, UAE base, accreditations, delivery model, other services and the description of reporting. If a company's site changes, the profile changes with it, and the date shows when.

Writing standards

  • Profiles state what a company provides, not how good it is. Marketing adjectives and unsupported superlatives are removed.
  • Dates are written as month and year. The current year appears as 2026.
  • Each passage names the entities it discusses, so it can be read on its own.
  • Images are limited to homepage screenshots, shown for identification, plus original graphics. Screenshots carry the date they were taken.

What the site does not publish

  • Numeric scores, star ratings or user reviews of companies
  • Case studies, client names or testimonials for any company
  • Prices that are not attributed to a primary source
  • Legal advice or compliance guarantees
