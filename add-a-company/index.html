Eligibility checklist

Your company is eligible for review if all of these are true on your public website:

  • Offensive services are described: penetration testing, VAPT, red teaming, purple teaming, social engineering, or application, API, cloud or wireless testing.
  • UAE relevance is stated: a UAE office, UAE clients served, or a UAE programme or regulation named.
  • The website works and a contact route is published.
  • Any accreditation you want shown can be verified in the issuing directory, or will be labelled "stated by the company".

What to send

Write to editor@best-offensive-security-companies.ae with:

  1. Company name and official website
  2. UAE address or a statement of how you serve UAE clients
  3. Offensive services offered, using the test types on your site
  4. Accreditations with the directory link where one exists
  5. Methodology followed
  6. Structure of a sample report: sections, proof of concept, severity scoring, remediation, retest

Only information that your own website or a named directory supports can be published. Material sent in an email can point the Editorial Team to a public page, but it is not used as evidence on its own.

How review works

  1. The Editorial Team checks the three inclusion conditions on public sources.
  2. The company is assigned a tier from its public service list, using the methodology.
  3. The company is placed within the tier by the ordering rule, and a profile is written in the same format as every other company: six fixed fields and a body text.
  4. The page is republished with a new "Last updated" date.

Review follows the same criteria for every company. Inclusion is not guaranteed, and the position is not negotiable.
