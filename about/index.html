About This Ranking

Offensive Security Companies UAE is a comparison of providers that sell penetration testing, red teaming and related offensive services to organisations in the UAE. It exists to help a buyer shortlist a provider by testing scope, reporting and delivery model.

This is an independent comparison compiled by the Editorial Team from public sources, with no paid placements.

Last updated: November 2026

On this page

  1. What you will find here
  2. How the Editorial Team works
  3. What this site does not do

What you will find here

  • A ranking of ten companies, grouped into three tiers. Each profile has the same six fields: UAE base, core offensive services, accreditations, delivery model, other services and best for.
  • Seven guides that explain the discipline: what an offensive security company is, how testing types differ, which UAE rules drive testing, how to choose a provider, what certifications mean, what drives cost, and how crypto testing differs from forensics.
  • Trust pages: methodology, editorial policy, sources, terms and contact.

The ten companies were chosen because each publicly describes offensive services and states UAE relevance. They are grouped by how much of their stated offering is hands-on testing, from offensive-only specialists to managed-security providers with a testing line. The grouping describes service mix. It is not a measure of quality, and it is not a ranking of results.

How the Editorial Team works

The Editorial Team collects facts from each company's own website and from the directories that issue accreditations. Every statement is labelled by its level of evidence: "stated by the company" or "listed in a directory, checked November 2026". What a company does not state is shown as "Not stated".

Regulations and standards are cited from the regulator's or standards body's own pages. Where a primary page does not mention penetration testing, the guides say so rather than infer a requirement.

The methodology sets out the tier model, the six criteria and the ordering rule. The editorial policy sets out how facts are sourced and corrected.

What this site does not do

  • It does not test the companies' services. Profiles describe what companies state publicly.
  • It does not publish numeric scores, reviews or ratings.
  • It does not offer penetration testing or sell services.
  • It does not give legal advice. The guides summarise regulator pages for orientation; read the full text before you rely on a rule.

If you find an error, write to the Editorial Team at editor@best-offensive-security-companies.ae. If you run an offensive security company and want to be reviewed, see Add a company.
